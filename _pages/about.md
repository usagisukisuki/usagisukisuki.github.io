  <h1 class="hp-name">Sota Kato<span class="hp-name-ja">加藤 聡太</span></h1>
  <p class="hp-lead">I am a researcher in computer vision and machine learning. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from Meijo University. I am interested in challenges and applications of real-world data in deep learning across diverse domains. My past research includes image recognition in cell biology and medical imaging, as well as learning under class imbalance. Recently, I have been exploring real-time models for low-TOPS hardware and methods to leverage knowledge from foundation and large-scale models.</p>
